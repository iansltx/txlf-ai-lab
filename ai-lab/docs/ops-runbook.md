# Operations Runbook

## Pouring schedule

We pour Tuesday through Thursday. Wax is heated to 185°F, fragrance is added at 165°F, and we pour at 140°F into room-temperature vessels. Candles cure for 14 days before they can be sold — no exceptions, even during the holiday rush.

## Summer shipping protocol

From May 15 to September 30, all outbound packages ship Monday through Wednesday only, so nothing sits in a hot warehouse over a weekend. Orders to Arizona, Nevada, and Texas destinations include a cold pack when the destination forecast is above 95°F. If a customer reports a melted candle, we replace it free of charge and ask them to recycle the vessel — no return shipment needed.

## Packing standard

Each candle is wrapped in one sheet of honeycomb paper, nested in shredded kraft fill, with a lid sticker to keep dust out. Boxes get a fragile stamp on two sides. Wax melts ship in padded mailers, maximum four clamshells per mailer.

## When the wick supplier is out of stock

Our primary wick is the CD-16 from our supplier in Georgia. If it is backordered, the approved substitute is the CD-18 with the fragrance load dropped by 1% — and only for the 9 oz tumblers. Do not substitute wicks in the 7 oz seasonal vessels; pause that production line instead.

## Escalation

Anything involving a burn injury claim, a chargeback over $200, or a wholesale account dispute goes directly to the owner the same day. Everything else can wait for the Tuesday production meeting.
