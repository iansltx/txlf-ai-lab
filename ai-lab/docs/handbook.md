# Kiln & Wick Candle Co. — Employee Handbook

## About the company

Kiln & Wick Candle Co. is a small-batch candle studio founded in 2019 in San Antonio, Texas. We pour every candle by hand in a converted print shop on the near-west side. The team is six people: two full-time chandlers, two part-time production assistants, one shipping lead, and the owner.

## Studio hours

The studio operates Tuesday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are closed Sundays and Mondays for equipment maintenance and wax deliveries. During the holiday rush (November 1 through December 23), Saturday hours extend to 7:00 PM.

## Returns and exchanges

Unlit candles in original packaging may be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Once a candle has been lit, we cannot accept a return, but we will replace any candle with a manufacturing defect — tunneling, a clogged wick, or cracked glass — within 90 days of purchase. Customers should email care@kilnandwick.example with a photo of the defect.

## Wholesale accounts

Wholesale pricing starts at 50 units per order with a 40% discount off retail. Wholesale orders require a 3-week lead time during most of the year and 5 weeks during the holiday rush. New wholesale accounts must be approved by the owner and place a minimum first order of $500.

## The wax we use

All Kiln & Wick candles use a coconut-soy blend: 70% coconut wax, 30% American-grown soy. We do not use paraffin in any product. Wicks are unbleached cotton with a paper core, and every fragrance oil is phthalate-free.
