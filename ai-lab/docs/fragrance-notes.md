# Fragrance Notes Reference

## How we describe scent

Every Kiln & Wick fragrance is described in three layers. Top notes are what you smell first; they fade within the first hour. Middle notes carry the body of the scent. Base notes anchor the candle and linger after it is extinguished.

## Desperado Dusk

Our best-seller since 2022. Top: bergamot and a whisper of smoked black tea. Middle: leather and dried tobacco leaf. Base: cedarwood, amber, and vanilla bean. Fragrance load is 9%.

## Porch Light

Top: lemon peel and fresh basil. Middle: honeysuckle and white tea. Base: light musk. Fragrance load is 8%. Porch Light is our lightest throw and the one we recommend for small rooms and bedside tables.

## Marfa Morning

Top: juniper berry and sage. Middle: desert lavender and chaparral. Base: sandalwood and rain accord. Fragrance load is 10% — our strongest throw, designed for open-plan spaces.

## Cast Iron

Top: black pepper. Middle: clove and charred oak. Base: dark amber, cacao, and a trace of campfire smoke. Cast Iron is the only scent we pour in a 12 oz option during the winter drop.
