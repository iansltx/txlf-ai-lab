# Product Catalog

## Core collection

The core collection comes in one size: a 9 oz amber glass tumbler with a burn time of 50 to 55 hours. Retail price is $28. Core scents are available year-round: Desperado Dusk, Porch Light, Marfa Morning, and Cast Iron.

## Seasonal drops

We release two limited runs each year. The summer drop (June) is 300 units per scent; the winter drop (October) is 500 units per scent. Seasonal candles come in a 7 oz matte white vessel, burn 38 to 42 hours, and retail for $24. Once a drop sells out, it does not return until the following year.

## Wax melts

Each clamshell of wax melts holds six 1 oz cubes and retails for $9. Melts use the same coconut-soy blend as our candles, and each cube scents a room for roughly 8 hours in a standard warmer.

## Care instructions

On the first burn, let the melt pool reach the edge of the vessel — about 2 hours for the 9 oz tumbler — to prevent tunneling. Trim the wick to 1/4 inch before every burn. Never burn a candle for more than 4 hours at a stretch.
